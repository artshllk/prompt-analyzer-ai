- `TRUE` the citation genuinely does not support the claim
- `FALSE` the citation is fine and we were wrong. This is the falseDefectRate number
- `DECAY` correct at publication, source changed since
- `UNSURE` could not tell in a reasonable time

**Nothing here says the claim is false.** The question is only whether the linked
page supports it.
