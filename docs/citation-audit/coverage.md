# Coverage: what fraction of an article can we speak about at all?

114 statistics from 11 stats-heavy articles the citation audit never touched.
**No judge calls, no verdicts, no human labelling.** For each cited source, one
question: can we honestly read it. Cost: 9 retrieval credits.

## The reframe that changes the answer

A source we cannot read is not coverage lost. The reason we cannot read it is
almost always a reason the **writer's reader** cannot read it either.

- A paywall stops them at the same wall.
- A dead link lands them on nothing, or on a product page.
- A live dashboard shows them a different number from the one in the sentence.

Each is a real credibility problem with an unambiguous fix, statable with total
confidence **without reading the page or judging anything**, so it carries no risk
of false accusation. Counted that way the answer is very different.

## The numbers

- **A verdict is possible on 34/114 = 29.8%.** This is the number that looks like a ceiling.
- **25/114 = 21.9% are refused but are findings anyway.** 20 paywalled, 3 dead, 2 live dashboard.
- **55/114 = 48.2% cite nothing at all.**

**Of every statistic that carries a citation, we can say something honest about 59 of 59. That is 100%.**
Either a verdict, or "your reader cannot verify this either", and the second needs
no judgement and cannot be a false accusation.

## The genre divide decides this too

| | Judgeable | Can say something |
|---|---|---|
| Best three publishers (n=37) | 65% | 89% |
| The other eight (n=77) | 13% | 34% |

searchenginejournal.com is 75% judgeable and 94% sayable. datareportal.com is 0%
and 0%, because it is a primary source publishing its own data and cites nobody.
That is not a failure of the tool: there is genuinely nothing to check.

## What this means

The ceiling on **verdicts** is real and sits near 30% of all statistics, driven
mostly by how little of this genre cites anything. The ceiling on **useful output**
is not the same number and is much higher, because refusing to judge a paywalled
source is itself the most actionable thing we can say about it.
