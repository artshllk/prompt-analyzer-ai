# Citation check: ahrefs.com

**Article** https://ahrefs.com/blog/seo-statistics/
**Checked** 2026-09-01 · 12 statistics sampled

Each entry is a statistic from the article and the link the article attached to it.
We fetched that link and asked one question: does this page contain this claim.

**We are not saying the claim is false.** A claim can be entirely correct and still
sit behind a link that does not show it. That is the commonest thing this finds, and
it is usually a source that moved or an article cited in place of the study it
reported on.

## The link does not show the claim

### a_ahrefs-01

**Claim, as published**

> 68% of online experiences begin with a search engine. ( BrightEdge )

**Link checked** https://videos.brightedge.com/research-report/BrightEdge_ChannelReport2019_FINAL.pdf

**Is `68%` anywhere on that page?** Yes, but attached to something else. See below.

**What we found** The source says 68% of trackable website traffic comes from Organic and Paid Search, not that 68% of online experiences begin with a search engine.

**Closest sentence on that page**

> BrightEdge Research found that Organic and Paid Search dominate websites’ traffic in 2019 - 68% of all trackable website traffic is sourced from Organic and Paid Search, vastly exceeding all other channels, including Display and Social Media.
