# Citation check: semrush.com

**Article** https://www.semrush.com/blog/content-marketing-statistics/
**Checked** 2026-09-01 · 14 statistics sampled

Each entry is a statistic from the article and the link the article attached to it.
We fetched that link and asked one question: does this page contain this claim.

**We are not saying the claim is false.** A claim can be entirely correct and still
sit behind a link that does not show it. That is the commonest thing this finds, and
it is usually a source that moved or an article cited in place of the study it
reported on.
