## DESCRIPTION

Any AI can reword your prompt. None of them ask what you actually meant.

Type your prompt in ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and press Alt+I (Option+I on a Mac). It gets improved right there in the box, in about a second. You never leave the chat.

WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT

• It asks before it rewrites. "Help me write a song" could mean a love song, a sad song, or something fun. Instead of guessing, it asks you one short question and gives you answers to tap. Then it rewrites, once, for what you actually meant.

• It knows the job. A coding prompt gets what an engineer would ask for: the code, the error, what "working" looks like. A cold email gets what a good copywriter would: a subject line, one reader, one clear ask. It does not use the same template for everything.

• It remembers your answers. Tell it once that you write for engineering leads and it stops asking. Next time it offers your usual answer first. It offers, it never assumes, and you can make it forget anything at any time.

• Your words are never lost. Press Esc to undo, or open Compare to see your original beside the new one and take yours back with one click.

• When your prompt is already clear, it says nothing and just improves it. No questions for the sake of questions.

HOW TO USE IT

1. Open ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini.
2. Type your rough prompt in the chat box.
3. Press Alt+I (Option+I on a Mac), or click the small ✦ Improve chip by the input.
4. Press Enter to send, or Esc to undo.

Nothing runs on its own. Your prompt is only sent to Deepclario when you press Improve.

We do not read your chats, your browsing, or anything else on the page.

If you make an account, we keep your prompt history so you can look back at it, and we remember your answers so we do not ask you the same thing twice. You can see all of it, and delete any of it, in your settings.

## PERMISSION JUSTIFICATIONS (the review form asks for these)

**storage**
To remember the user's account token and their preferences on their own device. Nothing is read from the browser beyond what the user themselves saved here.

**Host permission: deepclario.com**
To send the prompt the user has explicitly asked us to improve, and to receive the improved version back. This is the only server the extension talks to.

**Content scripts on chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com**
To read the prompt the user is writing in the chat box when they press Improve, and to write the improved version back into that same box. We do not read the conversation, the page, or anything the user has not asked us to improve.

**Remote code**
None. All code is bundled in the package.
